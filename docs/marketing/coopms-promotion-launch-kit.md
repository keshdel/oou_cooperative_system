## 14-Day Promotion Sprint

### Days 1-2: Build Target List

- List 100 cooperative societies in Nigeria.
- Prioritize staff coops, teachers coops, multipurpose coops, religious coops, and professional association coops.
- For each prospect, capture society name, contact person, phone/WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn profile, source, and notes.
- Add likely influencers: auditors, cooperative consultants, state cooperative officials, payroll/HR managers.

### Days 3-5: Warm Direct Outreach

- Send WhatsApp message 1 to all known contacts.
- Send email 1 to contacts with email addresses.
- Post LinkedIn founder-led content.
- Call the 10 most promising societies.
- Log every reply in HQ lead notes or activity.

### Days 6-9: Demo Push

- Follow up all replies.
- Ask direct question: "Would next Tuesday or Thursday work for a 20-minute demo?"
- Move responsive leads to `Contacted` or `Demo Booked`.
- Add demo date/time and presenter in HQ.

### Days 10-14: Conversion Push

- Send health check offer to non-responsive but qualified prospects.
- Send proposal to demo-completed prospects.
- Move prospects through: `Demo Completed`, `Proposal Sent`, `Negotiation`.
- Record setup fee, monthly subscription, expected close date, and decision reason.
