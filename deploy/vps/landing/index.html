📊 Double-Entry Accounting

Real books, not a savings spreadsheet

CoopMS is built on a genuine double-entry general ledger. Every savings deposit, loan disbursement, repayment, fee and expense posts a balanced journal entry automatically — so your trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow are always accurate and ready for audit, at any moment.

  • ✓ Automatic journal posting from every operational transaction — no manual bookkeeping.
  • ✓ Trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow that always tie out.
  • ✓ Full chart of accounts, manual journals, and period close to lock finalised months.
  • ✓ Bank accounts and reconciliation, with every figure traceable to its source document.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do we have to move off our current spreadsheets and accounting software?

Yes — that's the point. CoopMS replaces the spreadsheets and the separate accounting package with one system. We migrate your existing members, savings, loans and opening balances and reconcile them against your current trial balance, so nothing is lost.

Is this real accounting, or just a savings tracker?

It's real double-entry accounting. Every transaction posts a balanced journal entry, and you get a trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow that always tie out — the same statements your auditor expects.

Can members see their own balances?

Yes. Every member gets a secure self-service portal to view savings, share capital and loan balances, download statements, apply for loans and respond to guarantor requests — which dramatically cuts routine queries to your office.

Is our data secure and private?

Each society runs on its own secure site with its own data. Staff access is protected by two-factor authentication and role-based permissions, every action is logged in an audit trail, and finalised accounting periods can be locked.

How long does setup take?

Most societies go live within days. We handle the data migration and configuration; you review the reconciled figures and onboard your officers and members.

How much does it cost?

Pricing scales with your membership and the plan you choose. Tell us about your society and we'll send an exact quote along with a demo.
